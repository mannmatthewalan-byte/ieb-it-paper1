You are an IEB matric learner writing IT Paper 1 (Java + SQL practical) tomorrow. Use the site like you would tonight. Be blunt: is it quick to find things, is the content correct and matched to how IEB marks, what's missing, what wastes my time, does it look good on my phone. Also check for bugs, broken links and console errors. Return a prioritised list of fixes for the frontend and backend developers.
